Healthy weight for your height

This is an adult calculator (18+). For children and teens, BMI is read on age- and sex-specific growth charts, so the category above does not apply.

Over 65, a slightly higher BMI is often considered fine. Your doctor can tell you what suits you.

Adult BMI cutoffs are the same for women and men, although women usually carry more body fat at the same BMI.

Before you read too much into it

  • BMI is a quick screening number, not a diagnosis or medical advice.
  • It cannot tell muscle from fat, so athletes and very muscular people may score high.
  • It does not apply during pregnancy, and this calculator is for adults 18 and over.

Decimals: “.” or “,” both work.
