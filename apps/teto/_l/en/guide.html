Teto or Egen Test: what teto and egen mean and how to enjoy the test

Updated October 16, 2026

Teto and egen are two words that took over Korean social media and quickly spread far beyond it. This guide explains what they mean, where the trend came from, how the Teto or Egen test turns your everyday reactions into a teto and egen percentage, and how to get the most fun out of it with friends. Everything here is meant as light entertainment, not science, and the test works the same way for everyone, whatever their gender.

What do teto and egen mean?

Teto is short for testosterone and egen is short for estrogen, but in everyday slang they have very little to do with hormones. Teto describes an energy that is bold, direct and action-first: someone who decides quickly, speaks their mind and enjoys taking the lead. Egen describes an energy that is gentle, empathetic and sensitive: someone who notices how others feel and expresses emotions openly.

The words became popular because they gave people a quick, playful way to describe a vibe. Friends started labelling each other, comment sections filled with debates, and the combinations people used, like a teto woman or an egen man, made the joke even more fun because they broke old stereotypes. Today most people use the two words simply as energy styles, and nearly everyone has a bit of both.

Where the trend came from

The terms grew out of Korean online communities and short-form video in 2025, then spread through memes, variety shows and celebrity interviews. Part of the appeal was how easy the idea is to understand: you don't need to memorise sixteen letters or read a long report to know whether a moment felt teto or egen. Someone full of drive and quick decisions feels teto; someone full of warmth and quiet attention feels egen.

As the trend travelled, people in other countries recognised the same contrast that English speakers sometimes call masculine and feminine energy. This test keeps the original Korean words but treats them in a kind, gender-neutral way: anyone can be strongly teto, strongly egen, or somewhere in between.

How the test works

The test walks you through twelve ordinary everyday moments, the kind anyone runs into in a normal week. Each moment has four reactions, and every reaction leans a little or a lot toward teto or egen. There are no right or wrong answers and no hidden trick questions.

Your teto percentage is calculated only from your own answers: it is the share of teto points you collected out of everything you could have collected, and your egen percentage is the rest. Your type depends on two things: which way you leaned overall, and how strongly you leaned. The scoring is fixed, so the same answers always give the same result, and we checked every possible combination of answers so that each type has a fair chance of coming up.

  • Twelve short everyday situations with four choices each.
  • A teto vs egen gauge calculated from your own answers.
  • A result with a description, strengths, something to watch out for and an energy tip.
  • A best match and a rival, shown just for fun.
  • About two minutes, on any phone, with no sign-up.

It's not science, and it's not about gender

It's worth saying clearly: this is a fun quiz, not a psychological assessment, a medical test or a statement about anyone's body or identity. Real people change with their mood, their surroundings and the people they are with. You may feel very teto at work and very egen with your closest friends, and both are completely normal.

Short descriptions of ourselves often feel surprisingly accurate because they are written to be relatable, an effect psychologists call the Barnum effect. Enjoy your result as a snapshot of how you answered today, not a label you have to live up to. Neither energy is better than the other: the world needs people who charge ahead and people who notice who is being left behind.

Playing with friends and tips for honest answers

The test is most fun in a group. Before anyone starts, try predicting each friend's teto percentage, then compare. Couples, roommates and coworkers often discover that the person they thought was pure teto has a surprisingly soft side, or the other way around. After your result you can share a link that shows only your type, so friends can see it without any spoilers before they play.

For a result that really feels like you, answer with your first honest reaction instead of the one you think sounds cool or kind. Think about what you actually did the last time something similar happened. If you are in an unusual mood, take the test again another day and see whether your mix moves. Most of all, keep it light: laugh at the results, be kind about each other's types and enjoy the conversation it starts.
