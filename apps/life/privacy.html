Privacy Policy

My Life, Animated (the "Service") respects your privacy and handles only the minimum information necessary, as described below.

1. What you enter

The Service works without sign-up or login. The name or nickname, birth year and month, moments and years you pick, and any moment you write yourself are used in your browser to draw the animation. Your last answers may be kept in this browser’s local storage so you can edit them next time; you can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

2. Short share links

When a film finishes, the answers above are saved on our server (Supabase) under a random ID, so that a short link (?s=…) can open the same film. Anyone who has the link can watch it, so leave out anything you don’t want others to see. No email, account or device details are stored with it; your IP address is used only as a one-way hash (md5) to limit abuse. If the server can’t be reached, the answers are packed into the link itself (#d=…) instead.

3. Saving the video

"Save as video" uses your browser’s recording feature to create the video on your device. The video is not uploaded anywhere and only leaves your device when you download or share it yourself.

4. Ratings, hearts and usage statistics

If you leave a star rating, only the rating and a hash (md5) of a random value created in your browser are stored, to prevent duplicate ratings. Hearts and visits are counted per app; so that the same person isn’t counted twice within 30 seconds, a one-way hash of your IP address is kept for a short time. We also count daily totals of page views and finished films by date and language.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The Service may use cookies to serve ads and understand how the Service is used. You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may be limited if you do.

6. Advertising (Google AdSense)

The Service shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Learn more and change your preferences in Google Ad Settings.

7. Analytics (Google Analytics)

The Service may use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand visitor numbers and traffic sources. This information is used only for statistics and does not identify you. The names and moments you enter are never sent to analytics.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact the site operator.

9. Effective date

This policy is effective as of September 26, 2026.
