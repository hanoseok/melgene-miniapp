You are upbeat, curious and full of ideas. You find the fun in almost anything, you bounce back quickly, and your energy lifts everyone around you. New places, plans and possibilities light you up. Because there is always something exciting next, you can find it hard to stay with one thing, or with difficult feelings, for long. At your best you combine your joy with focus and depth, and you discover that being present can feel even better than the next plan.

💪 Your strengths

  • Bringing energy and fun to any group
  • Coming up with creative solutions fast
  • Bouncing back from setbacks

🌱 Tips for growth

  1. Finish one plan before starting the next, and enjoy that finished feeling.
  2. When something hurts, let yourself feel it for a moment before looking for a distraction.
  3. Try a slow, quiet hour now and then. Calm can be its own kind of adventure.
When you grow, you borrow from 🔭 Type 5 The Investigator

In growth you slow down, focus deeply and enjoy quiet, thoughtful moments.
