You are loyal, responsible and quick to spot what could go wrong, which makes you a great planner and a trustworthy friend. You take commitments seriously and care deeply about your people and communities. Because you think through every scenario, your mind can get busy with doubts, and it may take you a while to trust others, or yourself. At your best you are brave and steady, and you trust your own judgment as much as you trust the people you love.

💪 Your strengths

  • Spotting problems early and planning for them
  • Standing by friends through thick and thin
  • Bringing people together around a shared goal

🌱 Tips for growth

  1. When worry spirals, write down what is actually happening right now, not what might happen.
  2. Make one small decision a day without asking anyone, and notice it works out.
  3. Remember your track record. You have handled hard things before.
When you grow, you borrow from 🕊️ Type 9 The Peacemaker

In growth you relax, trust life a little more and find a calm, steady center.
