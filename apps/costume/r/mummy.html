This Halloween, you should beCozy Mummy

Patient, caring and the friend who holds everyone together.

You’re the one who quietly makes sure everyone’s okay. You remember the small things, you fix what’s broken, and you’re ready with a bandage — literally or emotionally. You’re patient and steady, with an old-soul charm and a love for classic, timeless things. People feel calmer just being around you.

Your party powers

  • Endless patience
  • Big caring heart
  • Rock-solid reliable

How to pull it off

  1. Wrap white gauze or strips of an old sheet over a white outfit.
  2. Leave one eye peeking out and let a few ends dangle loose.
  3. Dust the wraps with tea or coffee for an ancient look.
