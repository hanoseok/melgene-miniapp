This Halloween, you should bePumpkin King

Warm, bright and the heart of the party — king or queen of Halloween.

You light up every room like a lantern. You love bringing people together, you remember everyone’s name, and you make sure nobody feels left out. Parties feel more alive when you’re there, and you’re often the one who organizes them. Your warmth is contagious — people leave your side feeling a little brighter.

Your party powers

  • Born host
  • Contagious warmth
  • Brings everyone together

How to pull it off

  1. An orange top or hoodie with a jack-o’-lantern face cut from black felt.
  2. Top it with a green leafy headband or a little crown.
  3. Carry a candy bucket and hand out treats to everyone.
