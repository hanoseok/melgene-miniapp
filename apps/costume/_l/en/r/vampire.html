This Halloween, you should beVelvet Vampire

Effortlessly elegant, a little dramatic, and the star of every night.

You were born for the night shift. You love a good entrance, you know your best angle, and you can turn a plain evening into a scene from a movie. People are drawn to your quiet confidence and that hint of mystery. You take style seriously, but you also take care of the people in your circle — once you’re loyal, you’re loyal forever.

Your party powers

  • Magnetic charm
  • Flawless style
  • Owns the night

How to pull it off

  1. A black outfit plus a cape (a dark bedsheet works) instantly says “count of the castle”.
  2. Slick your hair back and add a touch of red at the corner of the lips.
  3. Plastic fangs and a dramatic slow bow when you arrive.
