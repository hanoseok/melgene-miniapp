This Halloween, you should beBedsheet Ghost

Shy-cute, cozy and secretly the funniest one in the room.

You don’t need the spotlight to have a great time. You prefer cozy clothes, a few close friends and watching the party from a comfortable spot. People may underestimate you at first, but your quiet observations and sneaky jokes catch everyone off guard. You’re gentle, kind, and the kind of friend who makes others feel safe.

Your party powers

  • Gentle kindness
  • Sneaky humor
  • Great observer

How to pull it off

  1. One white bedsheet with two eye holes. Classic, comfy and done in five minutes.
  2. Add sunglasses or a tiny hat to make it unmistakably yours.
  3. Carry a little sign that says “boo” for the cutest photos.
