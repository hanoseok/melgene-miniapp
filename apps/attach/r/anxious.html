You care deeply and you notice everything: a shorter reply, a change in tone, a plan that slips. Being close to people matters a lot to you, so when a connection feels uncertain your mind can race to fill in the gaps. That sensitivity makes you warm, attentive and loyal. The worry is not a flaw; it is a sign of how much you value the people in your life. With a bit of reassurance and practice, that big heart can feel calm as well as close.

💪 Your strengths

  • Noticing how others feel before they say it
  • Showing love and appreciation generously
  • Putting real effort into keeping relationships alive

🌱 Tips for your relationships

  1. When worry spikes, pause before you send that extra message. Ask yourself what you know, not what you fear.
  2. Tell people what reassures you, like a quick “talk later” text. Clear needs are easier to meet than guesses.
  3. Build a life that fills you up on its own: hobbies, friends, quiet time. Security grows from many anchors, not one.
Feels easy with 🌳 Secure

A steady, consistent partner or friend helps your worries settle, and your warmth helps them open up.

Just for fun and self-reflection. This is a light quiz, not a psychological assessment or diagnosis. Attachment styles can shift over time and in different relationships. If relationships often feel painful, talking with a counselor can really help.
