💪 Your strengths

🌱 Tips for your relationships

  1. When you need space, say so and say when you will be back. “I need an evening, let’s talk tomorrow” keeps trust intact.
  2. Try sharing one small feeling a week with someone you trust. Closeness can grow in tiny steps.
  3. Notice the urge to pull away when things get good. Staying a moment longer is often where connection deepens.
