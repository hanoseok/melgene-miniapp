Privacy Policy

Would You Rather (the “Service”) respects your privacy and processes only the minimum information described below.

1. Information we collect

The Service works without sign-up or login and does not ask for your name, email or contact details. The following information may be processed while you use it.

2. Anonymous vote totals

When you tap an option, only the question ID and the option number (A or B) are sent to our server (Supabase) and added to a running total. We do not store who picked what, and the totals are shown publicly as percentages. To avoid counting you twice, the list of questions you voted on is kept only on your device (local storage).

3. Cookies and similar technologies

The Service may use cookies for advertising and to understand how it is used. You can refuse or delete cookies in your browser settings, although some features may not work as expected.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

The Service shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits. You can learn more and change your preferences in Google Ads Settings.

5. Analytics

The Service may use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand visitor numbers and traffic sources, and records daily totals of events such as page views, ratings, hearts and completed packs. This information is used only for statistics and does not identify you.

6. Share links

Links you share from the result screen contain the pack name and your picks (A/B) as short codes, so the recipient can answer the same questions and compare.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact the site operator.

8. Effective date

This policy is effective as of September 27, 2026.
